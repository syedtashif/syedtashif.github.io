Projects

AI Model Interpretability & Explainability Analysis

Ongoing research

Research project — XAI, transformers, sparse autoencoders

Investigating mechanistic interpretability methods on GPT-2 and Zephyr-7B, including bias-encoding neuron identification and sparse autoencoder training on residual-stream activations. Also explored how LLMs encode Indian socio-cultural concepts using representation engineering with contrastive prompt analysis and PCA-based direction extraction. Cultural knowledge encodes 3.1× stronger than social biases, with peak representation at layer 12 and validation accuracy above 0.94.

Validation accuracy 0.94+   Peak layer 12

NLP & vision — CLIP, multimodal learning, zero-shot

Used a contrast-vector approach working directly in CLIP's representational space to distinguish idiomatic from literal expressions, without fine-tuning or prompting. Built an adaptive image-ranking system that dynamically adjusts embeddings based on idiomaticity.
